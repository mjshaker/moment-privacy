# Moment Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This policy applies to **Moment — Your age, in motion**, version 1.0.1, a Chrome extension that replaces the new-tab page with a customizable age counter.

## Information Moment handles

You enter a date of birth and, optionally, a birth time. Moment also stores your appearance and display preferences, including typeface, palette, accent color, number format, decimal precision, visual style, and birthday-progress and daily-reminder settings.

Moment uses this information to calculate your age and progress toward your next birthday and to display the page according to your preferences. Information is used only for these features.

## Local storage and offline operation

When installed in Chrome, Moment stores this information locally in your browser profile using chrome.storage.local. Moment does not use chrome.storage.sync to synchronize it between devices. The extension works offline and makes no network requests.

The developer does not receive or have access to your stored birthday, birth time, or settings. The extension does not add its own encryption to this local storage; access depends on the protections of your device and Chrome profile.

## Sharing and tracking

Moment does not sell or transfer your information to third parties, use it for advertising, or use it to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, external fonts, or remotely hosted application code. It does not access browsing history, website content, contacts, passwords, payment information, or location.

## Permissions

Moment requests the storage permission to remember your birthday and preferences between tabs and browser sessions. Replacing the regular Chrome new-tab page is the extension's core function.

## Access, changes, and deletion

You can view and change your birthday, birth time, and display preferences through Make it yours. These settings remain locally until changed or removed. Uninstalling the extension removes the information it stores in Chrome's extension storage. There is no server-side copy for the developer to retain or delete.

## Chrome Web Store and support

Google separately handles its Chrome Web Store services, such as extension distribution, reviews, and developer-account services, under Google's policies. Those services are separate from Moment's local processing.

For questions about Moment, contact the developer using the publisher contact or support details shown on Moment's Chrome Web Store listing. Please avoid posting your birthday or other personal information in public reviews or support messages.

## Updates to this policy

The policy may be revised if the extension's behavior changes. The date above indicates the most recent revision.
